### 🛠️ What I'm about

- 🎧 **DJ** — Pioneer gear, rekordbox libraries, and a radio brand of my own. A hobby I keep levelling up at.
- 🚁 **FPV** — Freestyle and cinematic builds — soldering, tuning, flashing, and flying what I build.
- 🔌 **Maker** — Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi — custom DDNS boxes and 3D-printed cases to house them.
- 🌐 **Web Dev** — A connected suite of sites, Workers and Discord bots — self-hosted and self-built end to end.
